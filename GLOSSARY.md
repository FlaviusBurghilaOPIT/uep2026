# RemoteCare Pro Glossary

Stable, project-specific terminology for the RemoteCare Pro platform — a clinician web dashboard, a patient mobile app, and a shared backend.

## Terminology

**Assistant**:
The in-app AI chat feature through which a patient asks questions about their medications and recovery in back-and-forth conversation.
_Avoid_: chatbot, AI Recovery Assistant, "the chat", "the cat"
